Western Australia's finished live facial recognition trial scanned 1,380,882 faces and led to 122 arrests, the government says

From the archive (Nov 4, 2026): Final figures update the mid-trial count we reported in September.

By the AI in Criminal Justice Today newsroom · Published November 23, 2026
Story type

Jurisdiction

AI application

According to the government release cited by Biometric Update, the trial generated 292 alerts, 9 of which were incorrect, and identified 146 registered sex offenders. The government points to a 0.00065 percent false match rate as evidence of technical success. Biometric Update notes that no figures on repeat scans are provided and argues the scale raises questions about proportionality; authorities say an internal evaluation comes next.

What we know

  • The trial scanned 1,380,882 faces with 100 cameras at 43 locations (Biometric Update, citing a government release).
  • It generated 292 alerts, 9 of them incorrect, and led to 122 arrests (same).
  • The government cites a 0.00065 percent false match rate (same).

What remains unknown

  • How many of the scans were repeat scans of the same people.
  • The results of the promised internal evaluation.
