Missouri's governor orders 30-day deletion of plate-reader data and bars state employees from AI facial recognition on it

From the archive (Sep 27, 2026): House Democrats say legislation is still needed.

By the AI in Criminal Justice Today newsroom · Published November 23, 2026
Story type
Archive
Jurisdiction
United States, Missouri
AI application
automated license plate readers, facial recognition
AI framework
Not yet labeled

Under the order, collected license plate reader data must be deleted within 30 days of capture, according to the report, and Missouri government employees are now prohibited from using artificially intelligent facial recognition software to supplement it. House Democrats plan action when the legislature reconvenes in January.

What we know

  • Gov. Mike Kehoe signed the order on Sept. 16 (Missouri News Network).
  • Plate-reader data must be deleted within 30 days of capture (Missouri News Network).

What remains unknown

  • Whether lawmakers will write the order into law.
