The ACLU sues San Diego and its police department to end use of Flock license plate readers

From the archive (Sep 30, 2026): The suit argues the city's plate readers violate Article 1 of the California constitution.

The American Civil Liberties Union Foundation of San Diego and Imperial Counties announced a lawsuit against the city of San Diego and the San Diego Police Department over their use of Flock Safety automated license plate readers, KPBS reported on September 30, 2026. The ACLU represents the Partnership for the Advancement of New Americans and seeks a court order telling the city to end the use of ALPRs. San Diego ACLU legal director Frederick Carroll said the use violates Article 1 of the California state constitution. The police department said it cannot comment on pending litigation.

What we know

  • The American Civil Liberties Union Foundation of San Diego and Imperial Counties sued the city and SDPD over Flock plate readers (KPBS, September 30, 2026).
