Oregon lawmakers from both parties plan a 2027 push to ban Flock and other plate-reader cameras

From the archive (Oct 2, 2026): A 2026 law already caps police retention of plate-reader data at 30 days.

Oregon lawmakers passed Senate Bill 1516 earlier in 2026, limiting the amount of time police may hold license plate reader data to 30 days, the Oregon Capital Chronicle reported on Oct. 2, 2026 in a story republished by KLCC. Rep. Willy Chotzen, a Democrat, and Rep. Alek Skarlatos, a Republican, are pushing legislation for 2027 that could ban Flock and other plate-reader cameras. Senate Judiciary chair Floyd Prozanski has yet to commit to specifics, and Flock spokesperson Andrew Smith defended the technology, according to the report.
