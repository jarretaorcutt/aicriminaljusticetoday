Hertfordshire police take live facial recognition to three town centres during a national knife crime week

The force lists Watford High Street for Saturday, 21 November 2026, after earlier stops this week; its own table shows 1 alert across past deployments that report results.

Hertfordshire Constabulary says it is supporting Sceptre, the national week of action to tackle knife crime taking place from 16 to 22 November, and that live facial recognition is part of that work. The force's facial recognition page lists Watford High Street for Saturday, 21 November 2026, and says the week is funded through the Government-funded Knife Crime Concentration Fund.

The same page lists two earlier deployments in the week, in Hemel Hempstead town centre and on Waltham Cross High Street, each from 12pm to 8pm. Their watchlist sizes, alerts and faces seen had not been filled in when we read the page.

For past deployments that do report results, the force's table shows watchlists of between 840 and 1129 people and 1 alert in total, at a High Street, Watford deployment where 6,232 faces were seen. The largest count listed is 51,320 faces seen at Brookfield Shopping Centre, Cheshunt, with no alerts.

The force says watchlists are made up of people wanted by Hertfordshire Constabulary or the courts and/or subject to court orders they have failed to follow, and may include high risk missing people. It says non-matching captures are deleted immediately, data from alerts is kept for 24 hours, and footage from a mobile CCTV deployment is deleted within 31 days. Every alert is first reviewed by a specially trained officer. The force cites an independent study finding a false positive rate of less than 1 in 10,000 for a watchlist of 10,000 people.

The page is the force's own account. We read it through a text service because the site blocked a direct request, and we did not review the independent study it cites. No critics are quoted. Earlier today we reported on Nottinghamshire Police's facial recognition van.

What we know

  • Hertfordshire Constabulary lists a live facial recognition deployment on Watford High Street for Saturday, 21 November 2026, during Sceptre, the national knife crime week of 16 to 22 November (Hertfordshire Constabulary).
  • Earlier deployments this week were in Hemel Hempstead town centre and on Waltham Cross High Street, 12pm to 8pm; results are not yet posted (Hertfordshire Constabulary).
  • Past deployments with results show 1 alert in total; the largest listed count is 51,320 faces seen at Brookfield Shopping Centre, Cheshunt (Hertfordshire Constabulary).

What remains unknown

  • How many alerts, stops or arrests this week's three deployments produced.
  • How many people were on this week's watchlists.

Benefits and opportunities

  • The force publishes each deployment's place, time, watchlist size, alerts and faces seen.
  • Stated safeguards include immediate deletion of non-matches and officer review of every alert.

Risks and limitations

  • Large numbers of faces are scanned for very few alerts: 51,320 faces seen at one deployment produced no alerts.
  • The figures and safeguards are the force's own account, with no independent review quoted.
