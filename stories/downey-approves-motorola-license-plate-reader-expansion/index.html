Downey, California, approves a five-year, $532,026 deal to replace and expand police plate readers to 23 locations

From the archive (Oct 15, 2026): The council chose a Motorola Solutions system.

The Motorola Solutions system will cover 23 locations, including 13 existing sites and 10 additional locations, according to the paper. Police say the cameras have helped recover more than 1,450 stolen or wanted vehicles, and the staff report says data is not shared with federal agencies or law enforcement agencies outside California.
