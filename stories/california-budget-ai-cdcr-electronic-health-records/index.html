California budgets $2.2 million to add AI to its prison health records

From the archive (Sep 26, 2026): Funding is scheduled to rise to $4.5 million a year by 2028-29, with quarterly progress reports required.

California's enacted 2026-27 budget includes $2.2 million to add artificial-intelligence functionality to the California Department of Corrections and Rehabilitation's electronic health-record system, OACRA News & Policy Review reported on Sept. 26. Funding is scheduled to increase to $4.5 million annually by 2028-29.

The budget requires CDCR to provide quarterly progress updates on the project, according to the report. The Legislative Analyst's Office analysis it cites does not identify a vendor.
