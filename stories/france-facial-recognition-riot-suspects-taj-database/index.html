France will run riot suspects' social media images through its criminal records face database

From the archive (Oct 23, 2026): Facial-recognition lookups in the TAJ database rose from about 375,000 in 2019 to about one million in 2024.

Justice Minister Gérald Darmanin announced that investigators will cross-reference images scraped from TikTok, Instagram and Discord against the TAJ database to identify suspects in the riots that began October 17, Tech Times reported on Oct. 23. The riots left at least 2,400 people in custody and nearly 1,000 police officers and gendarmes injured, according to the report.

The operation is described as retrospective identification under Article R40-26 of the Code of Criminal Procedure; the TAJ holds up to nine million frontal photographs. TAJ consultations linked to facial recognition grew from approximately 375,000 in 2019 to roughly one million in 2024. A March 2026 Disclose investigation reported patrol officers using Cognitec facial recognition software on their phones; the gendarmerie denied the allegations.

What we know

  • Investigators will match social media images against the TAJ database (Tech Times).
  • The TAJ holds up to nine million frontal photographs (Tech Times).
  • Facial-recognition-linked TAJ lookups grew from about 375,000 in 2019 to about one million in 2024 (Tech Times).

What remains unknown

  • How much of the TAJ lookup count is authorized use; there is no public way to tell.
