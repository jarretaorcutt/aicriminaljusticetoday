ICE buys AI social media listening tools in a contract worth just under $1 million

From the archive (Oct 13, 2026): The vendor says the tools can't be used for facial recognition; the record doesn't show how ICE will use them.

By the AI in Criminal Justice Today newsroom · Published November 20, 2026

ICE awarded Carahsoft Technology Corporation a three-year social media management contract covering Hootsuite Enterprise and Talkwalker, with the award dated September 30, Biometric Update reported on Oct. 13. The award is potentially worth just shy of $1 million through September 2029.

ICE's five-page sole-source justification says the agency already has approximately two years of historical social media conversation data in the Hootsuite environment, according to the report. Carahsoft says the platform cannot be used for facial recognition and that investigative tracking would violate its terms; the record does not establish how ICE will use the tools.

What we know

  • The three-year award is dated September 30 (Biometric Update).
  • It is potentially worth just shy of $1 million through September 2029 (Biometric Update).
  • ICE has about two years of historical social media data in Hootsuite (Biometric Update).

What remains unknown

  • Whether ICE uses the tools for investigations or tracking.
