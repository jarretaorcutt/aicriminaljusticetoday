Rasa Legal uses AI to check expungement eligibility in about three minutes and cut lawyers' time per case to about five hours

From the archive (Aug 3, 2026): founder Noella Sudbury told NPR the startup has helped clear the records of 34,000 people in Utah, Arizona and Pennsylvania.

Rasa Legal's app can determine whether someone is eligible for expungement in just three minutes, and AI has cut the time it takes the startup's lawyers to file a petition by more than half, NPR reported on August 3, 2026. Founder Noella Sudbury said the 10 to 12 hours it used to take to help one person clear a record is down to about five hours. Since launching its app in 2022, Rasa has helped clear the records of 34,000 people in Utah, Arizona and Pennsylvania, and more than 90% of the petitions it files are approved, NPR reported. Rasa's lawyers review everything that is sent out because the technology has made mistakes, especially early on. Checking eligibility is free; an attorney consultation is $25 and full representation is a flat $500 per case.
