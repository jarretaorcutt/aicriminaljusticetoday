ICE awards Thomson Reuters a data and analytics deal worth up to $125 million after one bidder responded

From the archive (Sep 28, 2026): ICE had planned a sole-source award, then opened the work to competition.

By the AI in Criminal Justice Today newsroom · Published November 20, 2026

ICE awarded Thomson Reuters Special Services a five-year data and analytics agreement worth up to $125 million that runs through September 29, 2031, Biometric Update reported on Sept. 28, 2026. ICE dropped an earlier sole-source plan on August 12, and TRSS was the only company to submit an offer once the work was competed, according to the report. ICE's original justification described continuous monitoring of as many as one million people or entities and contemplated geolocation and license plate recognition data; the report says it is not shown whether that scope carried into the award.

What we know

  • The agreement is worth up to $125 million over five years (Biometric Update).
  • TRSS was the only company to submit an offer (Biometric Update).
  • ICE separately awarded Palantir a contract worth about $45.8 million in June (Biometric Update).

What remains unknown

  • Whether the one-million-person monitoring scope survived into the final award.
  • How much has been obligated.
