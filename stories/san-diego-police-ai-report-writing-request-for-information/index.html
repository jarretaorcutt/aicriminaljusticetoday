San Diego police explore AI to help officers write reports, a contract estimated at up to $3 million

From the archive (Oct 13, 2026): The department calls it an exploration phase; it barred non-approved AI report tools in December.

By the AI in Criminal Justice Today newsroom · Published November 20, 2026

The San Diego Police Department issued a request for information on July 31 for a program that uses AI to help officers and investigators write police reports, with an estimated contract value between $500,000 and $3 million, the Los Angeles Times reported on Oct. 13. The department said it is in an exploration phase and would do outreach to elected officials and community members if it pursues a program.

In December, the department had prohibited officers from using non-department-approved AI platforms to write reports. Nearby Chula Vista police already use AI to write reports, according to the Times.
