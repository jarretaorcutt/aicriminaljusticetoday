Federal judiciary's AI task force aims to release final guidance by year's end

From the archive (Sep 18, 2026): The group formed after two federal judges said AI use by staff led to error-riddled opinions.

The federal judiciary's AI task force aims to share its final guidance by the end of the year, the ABA Journal reported on Sept. 18. Judge Sidney Thomas of the 9th U.S. Circuit Court of Appeals chairs the group, and its interim guidance already addresses judges' use of the tools.

The task force began after two federal judges revealed in October 2025 that AI use by their staff led to error-riddled opinions, according to the report.

What we know

  • Judge Sidney Thomas chairs the task force (ABA Journal).
  • Interim guidance already addresses judges' use of AI (ABA Journal).
  • The effort followed two judges' October 2025 disclosure of AI-caused errors in opinions (ABA Journal).

What remains unknown

  • What the final guidance will require.
