Santa Fe, Argentina, opens a prison with AI monitoring, facial recognition and voice ID that cuts off calls when someone else speaks

From the archive (Sep 3, 2026): The province says 25 million of the 80 million dollars spent on Penitentiary Unit No. 9 in Recreo went to technology.

Santa Fe province inaugurated Penitentiary Unit No. 9 in Recreo, a prison with 891 places, the provincial government said on 3 September 2026. Its voice identification system recognizes the detainee and authorized callers and automatically cuts the call if another person speaks, the release said, and AI monitoring and facial recognition are used to detect wanted people, visitors and possible risk situations. Governor Maximiliano Pullaro called it a prison "Lince", after the AI camera system used in Rosario. The province said its total investment reached 80 million dollars, of which 25 million went to technology. The release is in Spanish; this summary is our translation.

What we know

  • The prison has 891 places (Santa Fe government).
  • Voice identification cuts a call automatically if another person speaks (Santa Fe government).
  • 25 million of 80 million dollars went to technology (Santa Fe government).

What remains unknown

  • How often the voice system cuts off calls by mistake, and how a detainee can challenge it.
