The Ban Flock Act, H.R. 10691, would bar federal agencies from plate-reader systems, including ones on drones, without congressional authorization

From the archive (Oct 13, 2026): The bill would also tie aid from five federal departments to state and local limits.

Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez (D-NY) introduced H.R. 10691, the Ban Flock Act, on October 1, 2026, DroneLife reported on October 13. Under Section 3, federal agencies would be prohibited from acquiring, possessing, accessing or using ALPR systems without specific congressional authorization, and the bill's definition of captured plate data includes data from drones. Section 4 would condition federal financial assistance from five departments, including Justice, Transportation, and Homeland Security, on state and local limits. DroneLife notes the bill does not propose a general ban on drones or drone-as-first-responder programs, and that it remains proposed legislation and has not become law.

What we know

  • H.R. 10691 was introduced on October 1, 2026 (DroneLife).
  • It would bar federal agency ALPR use without authorization and covers drone-captured plate data (DroneLife).

What remains unknown

  • Whether any committee has taken up the bill.
