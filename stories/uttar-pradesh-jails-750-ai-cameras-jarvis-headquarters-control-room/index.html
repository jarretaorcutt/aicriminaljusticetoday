Uttar Pradesh links over 750 AI-enabled jail cameras to its prison headquarters and uses an AI system called JARVIS to monitor prisons

From the archive (Oct 19, 2026): The state's prison CCTV network grew from around 4,900 cameras in June 2025 to more than 7,000, Hindustan Times reported.

Uttar Pradesh expanded CCTV coverage across its prison network from around 4,900 cameras in June 2025 to more than 7,000, Hindustan Times reported on Oct. 19, 2026, citing official details from the chief minister's media cell. The network includes over 750 AI-enabled cameras linked directly to the headquarters control room, and an AI-based system called JARVIS is being used to monitor prison premises, the media cell said. The state also introduced an e-Prisons Early Release Processing Module for premature release cases and set up digital libraries in 74 jails.
