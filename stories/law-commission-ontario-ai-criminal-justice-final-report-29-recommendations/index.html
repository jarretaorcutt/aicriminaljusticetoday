Law Commission of Ontario issues 29 recommendations on AI in criminal justice

From the archive (Oct 22, 2026): They include provincial legislation, risk assessments for police AI and resources for accused people to respond when AI is used.

The Law Commission of Ontario released its final report on AI in criminal justice on Oct. 22, 2026. Its 29 recommendations propose strategies and safeguards for responsible use of AI by police, Crowns, courts and other institutions, the commission said.

The recommendations include new provincial legislation, standardized risk assessments for police AI systems, and resources so accused persons can effectively respond when AI is used in their case.
