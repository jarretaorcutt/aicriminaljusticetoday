Western Australia police report 79 arrests from a live facial recognition trial that scanned 905,117 faces

From the archive (Sep 22, 2026): Every false match in the trial misidentified a person with darker skin, the report says. Police say an officer confirms each match.

Western Australia Police say their live facial recognition trial was deployed 77 times and scanned 905,117 faces, producing 79 arrests over the first three months, 36 of them for outstanding warrants, Biometric Update reported on Sept. 22. The technology is provided by NEC.

The false alert rate was 0.0009 per cent, and all of the false matches misidentified people with darker skin tones, according to the report; police say the eight people were questioned but not arrested. Templates for non-matches are deleted immediately, police said. Formal evaluations are still to come.
