Apex, North Carolina, reconsiders its Flock plate-reader pilot after public-records findings

From the archive (Sep 29, 2026): Two federal agencies had access to the town's data as of March, according to records cited by INDY Week.

Apex police began using Flock cameras in October 2025 as a yearlong pilot that ends in January 2027, and the town council's Public Safety Committee will make a recommendation on its future, INDY Week reported on Sept. 29, 2026. Public records showed Apex shared data with about 1,000 organizations and that two federal agencies had access as of March; the police chief said that access was revoked. The outlet reports a 30-day retention limit and that about 13% of searches listed "APEX CASE" instead of a case number. Options include keeping Flock, switching to Axon or stopping plate-reader use.

What we know

  • The Flock pilot runs from October 2025 to January 2027 (INDY Week).
  • Two federal agencies had access to Apex's data as of March (INDY Week, citing public records).
  • About 13% of searches listed "APEX CASE" rather than a case number (INDY Week).

What remains unknown

  • The committee's recommendation.
