Pakistan's Sindh province inaugurates an AI-based CCTV and command and control system at Malir Jail and plans it for all its prisons

The Inspector General of Prisons said the system will keep records of visitors, The News reported on Nov. 19, 2026. No vendor, cost or data rules were reported.

Sindh Prisons Minister Ali Hassan Zardari has inaugurated an artificial intelligence (AI)-based CCTV surveillance and command and control system at Malir Jail, the Karachi newspaper The News reported on Nov. 19, 2026.

Inspector General of Prisons Fida Hussain told reporters the system is equipped with artificial intelligence and would maintain records of visitors coming to the prison to meet inmates, according to The News. He said it would help authorities keep track of visitors and monitor activities at the jail more effectively.

Hussain said the system would not remain limited to Malir Jail but would gradually be installed in all prisons across the province, and that the monitoring facilities would also be accessible to the prisons minister and prison administration, the paper reports. The minister said modern monitoring was essential to strengthen security, improve transparency and ensure effective supervision of prison administration.

The report does not name a supplier or a cost, and it does not describe what the artificial intelligence does beyond keeping visitor records, or what rules apply to the footage and records.
