San Diego Councilmember Stephen Whitburn, a past supporter, calls for the city to turn off its Flock cameras

From the archive (Oct 13, 2026): The police chief said the technology has helped solve more than 900 cases.

San Diego City Councilmember Stephen Whitburn called for the city to remove its Flock license plate reader cameras after repeatedly voting to keep them, saying national events had changed his mind, KPBS reported on October 13, 2026. "Let's turn off the cameras and cancel the Flock contract," he said. In December 2025, the council voted 5-3 to continue using ALPR technology, and a council majority later left the $2 million ALPR contract with Flock untouched in the budget. A pending ACLU lawsuit says the city runs at least 500 ALPR cameras. Police Chief Scott Wahl said the technology has helped solve more than 900 cases.
