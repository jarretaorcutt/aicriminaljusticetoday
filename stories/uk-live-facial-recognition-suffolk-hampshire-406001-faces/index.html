More UK forces roll out live facial recognition as Hampshire reports 406,001 faces scanned in a year

Suffolk Constabulary announced live facial recognition deployments in Lowestoft, Bury St Edmunds and Ipswich using equipment loaned by Bedfordshire Police, Biometric Update reported on Oct. 30, 2026. The outlet reports that Hampshire and Isle of Wight Constabulary scanned 406,001 faces during 20 LFR deployments over the past year. The government has said the Police Reform Bill would establish a framework for biometric and overt surveillance technologies, according to the report.
