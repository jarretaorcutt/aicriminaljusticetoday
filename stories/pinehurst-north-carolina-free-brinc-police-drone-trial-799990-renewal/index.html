Pinehurst, North Carolina, approves a free one-year police drone trial that rolls into five paid years unless it gives notice

Motorola's paperwork lists five paid years at $159,998 each after the free year, a $799,990 total, DroneXL reports from the council packet.

The Village of Pinehurst Council approved a one-year trial of a Drone as First Responder program for the Pinehurst Police Department at its Tuesday, November 17 meeting, DroneXL reported on Nov. 20, 2026. The village of about 18,700 residents gets two BRINC Responder drones and their docking stations through Motorola Solutions, with no subscription fee for the first 12 months.

According to the council packet as reported by DroneXL, Motorola's addendum lets Pinehurst end the subscription without penalty by giving notice up to 30 days before the first paid year. Without that notice, five paid years at $159,998 each follow, and Motorola's quote puts the total at $799,990. The staff memo says staff will plan around the notice provision and report findings to the council before the deadline.

Police Chief Glen Webb's slides estimate a drone would be first on scene 72% of the time, DroneXL reports; the village's own scorecard lists the police 90th percentile response time to high-priority calls as 3:29. The village says the drones will answer specific calls and will not conduct routine aerial patrols or general surveillance.

What we know

  • The council approved a one-year trial of two BRINC Responder drones through Motorola Solutions on November 17 (DroneXL).
  • Five paid years at $159,998 each follow unless the village gives notice in time; the quote totals $799,990 (DroneXL).
  • The village says the drones will not conduct routine aerial patrols or general surveillance (DroneXL).

What remains unknown

  • The text of the drone use policy, which was not in the packet (DroneXL).
  • Whether the village will continue past the free year.

Benefits and opportunities

  • The chief cited officer safety and live information for officers on the way to calls (DroneXL).

Risks and limitations

  • The paid years start automatically unless the village gives notice in time (DroneXL).
