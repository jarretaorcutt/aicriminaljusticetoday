Colorado's Custer County Sheriff's Office ends its Flock plate-reader contract and removes the cameras

From the archive (Oct 7, 2026): The office says it doesn't plan to install another plate-reader system.

The Custer County Sheriff's Office in Colorado ended its contract with Flock Safety and a contractor removed the remaining cameras, KRDO reported on Oct. 7, 2026. The office initially operated three Flock cameras, notified Flock on March 24 that it wanted to cancel, and kept data for 30 days before automatic deletion, according to the report. Sheriff Smith credited the system with helping in a 2023 triple homicide case but said he was troubled the tool had been misused elsewhere.
