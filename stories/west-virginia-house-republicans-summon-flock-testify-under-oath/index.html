West Virginia House Republicans ask Flock to testify under oath after calling its written answers incomplete

From the archive (Oct 19, 2026): Four committee members set a Nov. 9 hearing and had earlier threatened subpoenas.

Four Republican members of the West Virginia House Government Organization Committee, including Chairman Chris Phillips, asked Flock Safety to send a representative to testify under oath at the committee's Nov. 9, 2026 meeting, The Inter-Mountain reported on Oct. 19. In an Oct. 5 letter the lawmakers had threatened to use the committee's subpoena powers, and Flock was given an Oct. 9 deadline to respond. Flock's chief legal officer, Dan Haley, wrote that the company "has worked in good faith to answer your questions." The lawmakers say the company has not said which "non-U.S. vendor" it sends customer images to, according to the report.
