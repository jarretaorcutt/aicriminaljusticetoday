Madison Heights, Michigan, disconnects its city-run Flock cameras and will let the contract lapse

From the archive (Oct 11, 2026): Officials say Flock couldn't provide the safeguard assurances the city wanted.

Madison Heights officials said the city will disconnect the Flock cameras it operates and will not renew its contract with the company when it expires, CBS Detroit reported on Oct. 11, 2026. The city's three cameras have been covered and collected no new data since Sept. 23, and officials said Flock has been unable to provide assurances about safeguards. CBS Detroit cites a Washington Post report that 46 officers have been charged or accused of misusing the technology nationally.
