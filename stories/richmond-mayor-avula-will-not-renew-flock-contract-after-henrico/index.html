Richmond's mayor says the city will not renew its Flock plate-reader contract, days after Henrico dropped Flock

From the archive (Oct 10, 2026): the current contract runs until June of 2027, The Richmonder reports.

The Richmonder reported on October 10, 2026 that Richmond Mayor Danny Avula announced in an Instagram video that the city will not move forward with its Flock automated license plate reader contract when it is up for renewal. The mayor's office says the contract is set to expire in June of 2027. The announcement came after neighboring Henrico County said it was ending its Flock contract immediately.

What we know

  • Mayor Danny Avula said the city will not renew the Flock contract (The Richmonder).
