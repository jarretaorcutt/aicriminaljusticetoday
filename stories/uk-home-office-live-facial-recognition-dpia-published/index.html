UK Home Office publishes its data protection assessment for live facial recognition

From the archive (Oct 22, 2026): The 62-page document covers the software, hardware and deployment processing.

The Home Office and Immigration Enforcement published a data protection impact assessment on 22 October 2026 covering live facial recognition software, hardware and associated operational processing, according to its GOV.UK page. The document is a redraft of an earlier template prepared for the live facial recognition proof of concept.

The Home Office says the assessment is meant to fulfil UK GDPR accountability requirements and demonstrate compliance. It is published as a 62-page PDF, reference 184.26; the page gives no deployment dates or locations.

What we know

  • The DPIA covers live facial recognition software, hardware and operational processing (GOV.UK).
  • It redrafts a template prepared for the proof of concept (GOV.UK).
  • It is a 62-page document published 22 October 2026 (GOV.UK).

What remains unknown

  • Where and when the system will be deployed.
