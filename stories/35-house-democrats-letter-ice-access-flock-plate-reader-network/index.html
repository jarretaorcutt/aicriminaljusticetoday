35 House Democrats ask ICE how it reaches Flock's plate-reader network through local police

From the archive (Oct 23, 2026): The letter set a Nov. 20 deadline for answers on legal authority and policy.

Reps. Raja Krishnamoorthi of Illinois and Brittany Pettersen of Colorado led 33 other House Democrats in a letter to Acting ICE Director David Venturella demanding answers about the agency's use of local police to reach Flock Safety's license plate reader network, Krishnamoorthi's office announced on Oct. 23, 2026. The release cites reporting of more than 4,000 searches of Flock camera data by state and local law enforcement for federal or immigration purposes, and says ICE has no formal contract with Flock governing that access. The lawmakers requested responses by November 20, 2026. The release also notes Krishnamoorthi's NO FLOCK Act, introduced with Republican Rep. Michael Cloud. This account comes from the letter's sponsor; ICE's position is not included.

What we know

  • 35 House Democrats signed a letter to ICE's acting director about access to Flock data (Rep. Krishnamoorthi's office, Oct. 23, 2026).
  • The letter requested responses by November 20, 2026 (Rep. Krishnamoorthi's office).

What remains unknown

  • Whether ICE answered by the deadline; we have not checked.
  • How many times ICE obtained Flock data and under what legal authority.
