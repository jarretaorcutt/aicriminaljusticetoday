Milford, Michigan, votes 7-0 to remove its remaining Flock plate-reader cameras

From the archive (Oct 5, 2026): The police chief cited problems with the vendor, not the cameras' effectiveness.

The Village of Milford council in Oakland County, Michigan, voted 7-0 not to renew its contract with Flock Safety, WDIV ClickOnDetroit reported in a story updated Oct. 6, 2026, so the remaining 10 Flock Safety license plate cameras will be removed. Milford approved 10 cameras in 2023 and added five more in 2024. Police Chief Scott Tarasiewicz said the issues were on the back end, and a resident petition gathered more than 600 signatures, according to the report.

What we know

  • The council voted 7-0 not to renew (WDIV).
  • A petition against renewal gathered more than 600 signatures (WDIV).

What remains unknown

  • Whether the village will buy another system.
