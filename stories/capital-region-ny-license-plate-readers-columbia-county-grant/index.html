New York's Capital Region splits over plate readers bought with state grants; one county's supervisors say they weren't told

From the archive (Oct 11, 2026): Columbia County's sheriff spent $97,129.58 of a state grant on 10 cameras, the Daily Gazette reports.

The Columbia County sheriff's office bought 10 cameras and installed them in October 2025 for $97,129.58 out of a $279,790 state grant, and supervisors had no idea the money would be used that way, the Daily Gazette reported on Oct. 11, 2026. The county attorney is reviewing the purchase. Saranac Lake voted to cancel its Flock contract, while the St. Johnsville police department operates four Flock cameras funded by a state grant, according to the report.
