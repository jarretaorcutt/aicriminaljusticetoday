UK names a permanent director for its £75m national police AI centre

From the archive (Oct 13, 2026): Projects include exploring AI to prepare and quality-check evidence case files.

Al Murray, who had served as interim Director of Police AI, has been appointed to the role on a substantive basis, Emergency Services Times reported on 13 October 2026. He is overseeing the transition of police AI work from the National Police Chiefs' Council to a national centre in the College of Policing, supported by £75m of Home Office funding over three years. Projects include exploring AI tools to prepare and quality-check case files of evidence, identify child sexual abuse images and analyse CCTV, according to the report.
