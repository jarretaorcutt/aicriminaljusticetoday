LA County Probation gives 10 youth at Camp Afflerbaugh a full day of AI and digital skills training

The department calls it the first training of its kind built for youth in its care; youth who return home before January 31, 2027 can get a free laptop.

The Los Angeles County Probation Department said in a November 25, 2026 news release that it worked with the county's Internal Services Department (ISD) and Department of Youth Development to give 10 youth from Camp Afflerbaugh a full day of digital literacy and artificial intelligence training. The session was held at ISD headquarters in Downey and was offered through the county's Delete The Divide initiative. The department describes it as the first training of its kind developed specifically for youth in Probation's care and the first of several planned sessions. According to the release, the hands-on workshops covered technologies such as ChatGPT, Gemini, small and large language models, and how to write effective prompts, and were designed to help youth apply the tools to education, vocational and personal goals. Participating youth who return home before January 31, 2027 will be eligible for a free laptop from ISD and Delete The Divide. Chief Probation Officer Guillermo Viera Rosa said access to technology and skills can open doors to education, employment and a stronger future. The release does not say how the training will be evaluated or how many youth will take part in later sessions.
