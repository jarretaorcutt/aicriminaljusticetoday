Indian police load 850 people's photos into facial recognition at a temple festival

From the archive (Oct 14, 2026): Kolhapur police say thefts dropped; the report notes an alert alone doesn't show anyone committed a crime.

By the AI in Criminal Justice Today newsroom · Published November 20, 2026
Story type

Jurisdiction

AI application
live facial recognition

Kolhapur police uploaded photographs of around 850 people with criminal records to a facial recognition system linked to cameras at the Shree Ambabai Mahalaxmi temple during Navratri, the Hindustan Times reported on Oct. 14. Of 125 cameras used for live monitoring, around 22 at entry and exit points have facial recognition, according to Superintendent of Police Neelotpal.

A private agency monitors the premises live, and plainclothes police respond to alerts. Police report a significant reduction in cases in and around the temple; the article notes that an alert alone does not establish that a person has committed an offence and gives no accuracy figures.

What we know

  • Photos of around 850 people were loaded into the system (Hindustan Times).
  • Around 22 of 125 cameras have facial recognition (Hindustan Times).
  • Police report fewer cases but give no comparison figures (Hindustan Times).

What remains unknown

  • How accurate the alerts are; no false-match figures were given.
