Bayfield County, Wisconsin, committee withdraws its call for a countywide Flock camera policy

From the archive (Oct 22, 2026): The county will first gather its existing surveillance policies; the sheriff runs six plate readers.

On Oct. 19 the Bayfield County Sheriff's Committee rescinded its recommendation for a countywide policy on Flock cameras, reversing a 3-2 decision made on Sept. 28, the Ashland Daily Press reported. The vote was a unanimous voice vote, and no recommendation was to go to the county board's Oct. 27 meeting. Deputy Administrator Kelly Westlund will gather all county policies on surveillance technology for review. The sheriff's office operates six automated license plate readers through Flock Safety, and Sheriff Tony Williams has questioned whether the board can set policy for his office. A committee member said about 350 people had signed a petition opposing the cameras.
