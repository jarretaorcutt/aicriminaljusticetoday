Orleans Parish sheriff signs a contract for AI to monitor jail calls and social media

From the archive (Aug 24, 2026): The deal with Bancroft Global costs $5,000 a month for six months, Fox 8 reported.

Orleans Parish Sheriff Michelle Woodfork signed a contract with Bancroft Global to use its AI and other tools to help secure the parish jail, including monitoring calls made in custody, Fox 8 (WVUE) reported on Aug. 24, 2026. The sheriff's office said it primarily aims to "connect the dots" on criminal networks and weed out corrupt deputies smuggling contraband. The contract says the AI can also help deputies investigate surveillance video, body-worn camera footage and suspect interviews. The partnership costs $5,000 a month for six months; Metropolitan Crime Commission President Rafael Goyeneche said the price is worth it, Fox 8 reported.
