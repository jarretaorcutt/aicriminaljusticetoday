ICE asks vendors about one AI system to analyze detainee calls, messages, video visits and cameras

From the archive (Oct 9, 2026): The request describes voice profiles built from recorded calls, including checks on who answers.

By the AI in Criminal Justice Today newsroom · Published November 22, 2026
Story type
Archive
Jurisdiction
United States
AI application
voice biometrics, call and message analysis, video analytics
AI framework
Not yet labeled

U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement posted a request for information for a single AI-powered system for detention facilities, Project Salt Box reported on Oct 09, 2026. Voice profiles would be built from calls ICE has already recorded, and cameras would be watched for behavior the work statement labels "isolation or withdrawal" and "unusual congregation." A human being must review the findings, and the work statement says output cannot be the only basis for enforcement or punishment. DHS keeps a public list of AI tools with 50 entries for ICE, and this system is not among them; ICE has reviewed 15 of its tools that were presumed to be high-impact and decided none of them were. A request for information is not a contract.

What we know

  • The request describes voice profiles built from already-recorded calls (Project Salt Box).
  • Output cannot be the only basis for enforcement or punishment, per the work statement (Project Salt Box).

What remains unknown

  • How a detainee or family member would learn about or challenge a wrong voice match.
  • Whether ICE will issue a solicitation or award a contract.
