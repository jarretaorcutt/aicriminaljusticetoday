Alexandria police still haven't decided who will review their plate-reader audits

From the archive (Oct 21, 2026): The city's Independent Policing Auditor post has been vacant since Oct. 3.

The Alexandria Police Department has not decided whether the city's Independent Policing Auditor or its Office of Internal Audit will have a role in reviewing its license plate reader audits, The Alexandria Brief reported on Oct. 21. The auditor's office has been vacant since Oct. 3.

The department said state law does not allow a traditional outside audit and did not name anyone who reviews its audits now. A National Policing Institute survey of 1,435 adults found independent audits were the safeguard most likely to raise public confidence, the Brief reported.
