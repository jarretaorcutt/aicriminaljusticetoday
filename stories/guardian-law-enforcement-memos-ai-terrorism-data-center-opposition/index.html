Police memos show agencies treating AI as both a terror tool and a target

From the archive (Oct 14, 2026): The Guardian reviewed 80+ law enforcement documents; they also show monitoring of online opposition to data centers.

By the AI in Criminal Justice Today newsroom · Published November 20, 2026
Story type

Jurisdiction

The Guardian reviewed more than 80 documents and memos distributed among federal, state and local law enforcement offices between 2021 and 2026, obtained by the transparency nonprofit Property of the People, it reported on Oct. 14. An FBI memo distributed by its Los Angeles division in May 2026 warned that threat actors will likely increase their use of generative AI tools for attack planning.

The documents also show police and intelligence officers monitoring online opposition to data centers among groups they describe as extremist, the Guardian reported, raising questions about whether that tracking reaches non-violent protest. The FBI did not respond to detailed questions.
