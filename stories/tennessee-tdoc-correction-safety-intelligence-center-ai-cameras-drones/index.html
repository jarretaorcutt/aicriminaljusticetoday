Tennessee prisons open an intelligence center using AI cameras, drones and counter-drone alerts against contraband

From the archive (Sep 29, 2026): The department says its drones logged more than 5,000 flights over the past year.

The Tennessee Department of Correction said its new Correction Safety Intelligence Center combines AI-powered tools, drones, and counter-drone technology, WKRN reported on Sep 29, 2026. The center uses AI-enabled camera systems that can alert staff when someone enters an unauthorized area and detect objects that may be weapons or cell phones, according to the department. Its drone program logged more than 5,000 flights and about 1,100 flight hours over the past year. TDOC Chief of Staff Elizabeth Foy called it a game changer; the department did not publish accuracy figures for the camera alerts.
