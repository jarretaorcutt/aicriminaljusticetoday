A Florida woman wrongly jailed for 13 days in a fatal-crash case testifies at a Senate hearing on Flock's AI camera network

From the archive (Sep 23, 2026): Charges were dropped after troopers found no crash damage on her car; the report does not say what led to her arrest.

Lindsey Isaacs of Palm Coast spent 13 days in jail after being wrongfully arrested by the Florida Highway Patrol over an October 2025 crash on Interstate 4 that left three people dead, FOX 35 Orlando reported on Sept. 23, 2026. Charges were dropped after a separate FHP team found zero crash damage on her vehicle, and she has filed a federal lawsuit against the agency. She testified before the Senate Judiciary Committee's Subcommittee on Crime and Counterterrorism at a hearing titled "Always Watching: Flock's Nationwide AI Surveillance Network." The report does not state what evidence or system led to her arrest.
