Texas comptroller bars state grant money for license plate readers; local and federal funds are still allowed

From the archive (Oct 14, 2026): The ban covers all future grants administered by the comptroller's office.

Texas Comptroller Don Huffines banned state grants for buying or contracting for automatic license plate readers, the Houston Chronicle reported on Oct 14, 2026. The ban applies to all future grants administered by the Texas Comptroller of Public Accounts, and the office's definition names vendors including Flock Safety, Motorola Solutions Inc., Axon Enterprise Inc. The ban does not stop law enforcement agencies from using city, county or federal funds. The paper notes it goes further than an earlier pause by Gov. Greg Abbott on state agency funding for Flock technology.

What we know

  • The ban applies to all future grants administered by the Texas Comptroller of Public Accounts (Houston Chronicle, Oct 14, 2026).
  • Agencies can still use city, county or federal funds (Houston Chronicle).
