Indiana prosecutor threatens to sue county leaders who want to cover Flock cameras

From the archive (Oct 20, 2026): The county council voted not to fund the contract; commissioners want to bag the six plate readers.

The Marshall County Council passed a resolution saying the county won't fund any more of its Flock Safety contract, citing privacy concerns, WVPE reported on Oct. 20. County commissioners want to cover or bag the six license plate readers for now.

County Prosecutor E. Nelson Chipman Jr., whose office holds the Flock contract, called bagging the cameras "tyrannical" and told commissioners to cease and desist, according to the report. The commissioners' attorney, Sean Sirisi, said there is a little bit of risk but he hopes it does not end up in court.

What we know

What remains unknown
