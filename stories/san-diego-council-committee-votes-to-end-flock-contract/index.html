A San Diego City Council committee voted to move toward ending the city's Flock plate-reader contract

From the archive (Oct 21, 2026): KPBS reports the full council would still have to approve the changes.

KPBS Midday Edition reported on October 21, 2026 that the budget and government efficiency committee, chaired by Councilmember Henry Foster, voted to approve his request to rescind the city's use policy on automated license plate readers, ask the mayor to terminate the contract, and amend the surveillance ordinance to prohibit the readers. KPBS metro reporter Andrew Bowen said the use-policy change would effectively end use immediately if approved by the full city council.

What we know

  • The committee approved Foster's three-part request (KPBS, Oct 21, 2026).
  • The use-policy change would take effect only if the full council approves it.

What remains unknown

  • How the full council voted.
