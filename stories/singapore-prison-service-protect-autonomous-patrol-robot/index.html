Singapore Prison Service trials PROTECT, a patrol robot officers can use to deploy irritant spray remotely

From the archive (Sep 3, 2026): The robot uses AI-enabled navigation and streams live video and audio to a control centre.

The Singapore Prison Service said on 3 September 2026 that it and HTX (Home Team Science and Technology Agency) developed PROTECT, an autonomous robotic unit. It can patrol autonomously along predefined routes using Artificial Intelligence-enabled navigation, and streams live video and audio to officers at the Correctional Unit Control Centre. If inmates fail to comply with instructions, an officer may remotely activate its interdiction capabilities, such as a Long-Range Acoustic Device, light distraction device or irritant spray, the service said; officers keep responsibility for all operational decisions.
