Kane County Sheriff begins removing five Flock cameras from the Mill Creek neighborhood after a residents' board vote

From the archive (Oct 6, 2026): the Mill Creek advisory board voted to request removal, Shaw Local reports.

Shaw Local reported on October 6, 2026 that the Kane County Sheriff's Office began taking down Flock license plate recognition cameras in the Mill Creek neighborhood on Oct. 6, after the Mill Creek SSA Advisory Board voted on Oct. 5 to request their removal. There are five cameras in total, and removal was expected to be finished the following week. The outlet describes it as the first instance of Flock being taken down in the county. The page does not describe the residents' specific concerns.

What we know

  • The Mill Creek SSA Advisory Board voted on Oct. 5 to request removal (Shaw Local).
  • Removal of five cameras began Oct. 6 and was expected to finish the next week (Shaw Local).

What remains unknown

  • Whether the removal is permanent and what happens to the cameras afterward.
