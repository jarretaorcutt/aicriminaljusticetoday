The Denver Gazette reported on October 8, 2026 that police in Aurora, Colorado turned off 12 Flock cameras at high schools on Tuesday after councilmembers raised concerns. The cameras were installed in the parking lots of five Aurora high schools in 2025, and the contract costs about $65,000. Police Chief Todd Chamberlain said Flock will remove them within three weeks unless the Aurora Public Schools board votes to begin its own contract. The police department attributed four recent arrests to the school cameras.
