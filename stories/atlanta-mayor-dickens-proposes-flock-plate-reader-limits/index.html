Atlanta's mayor proposes monthly audits and new limits on police use of Flock plate readers and cameras

From the archive (Sep 20, 2026): A police audit found less than 1% of uses potentially improper, with nearly 80 cases flagged.

Atlanta Mayor Andre Dickens released a policy proposal on Sept. 15 that would restrict how the Atlanta Police Department uses its Flock Safety license plate readers and cameras, WSB-TV reported on September 20, 2026. The proposal would audit officers' Flock use monthly, and the mayor wants it passed within 60 days. According to WSB-TV, a police audit found the share of potentially improper uses was less than 1%, with nearly 80 cases flagged as questionable. The department does not publicly share how many Flock cameras are in the city.
