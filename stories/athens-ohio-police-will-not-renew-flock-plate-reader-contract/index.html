Athens, Ohio police say they will not renew their Flock license plate reader contract

From the archive (Oct 7, 2026): the department keeps its six cameras until the contract ends in February 2027, the Athens County Independent reports.

The Athens County Independent reported on October 7, 2026 that the Athens Police Department will not renew its contract with Flock Safety for automated license plate reader cameras. The department currently uses six Flock cameras, the contract expires in February 2027, and the department will continue to use Flock through the length of the contract. The Athens County Sheriff's Office uses 10 cameras under a contract running until June 2028. Police Chief Nick Magruder is quoted: "We are not going to get into the ALPR business again."
