ICE buys integration software for its STELLA AI platform and claims 12 deployed AI applications

From the archive (Oct 6, 2026): DHS's July AI inventory still listed the platform's assistant as pre-deployment, Biometric Update notes.

ICE awarded a delivery order worth up to almost $4 million for the MuleSoft Anypoint Platform as part of a common data and AI architecture built around its STELLA platform, Biometric Update reported on Oct. 6, 2026, citing procurement records. ICE describes a portfolio of 12 deployed AI applications but does not identify them, and DHS's July AI inventory still listed the platform's assistant as pre-deployment, according to the report. ICE also awarded a deal worth up to $24.79 million to ASRC Federal for data-quality services.

What we know

  • ICE ordered MuleSoft integration software worth up to almost $4 million (Biometric Update).
  • ICE claims 12 deployed AI applications, which it does not identify (Biometric Update).
