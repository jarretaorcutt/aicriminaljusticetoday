Winter Park, Florida, switches off its Flock and other plate-reader cameras for at least 90 days

From the archive (Sep 28, 2026): The police chief says a new policy may not be ready until early 2027.

Winter Park Police Chief Tim Volkerson confirmed the department's Flock cameras and other automated license plate readers are no longer in use after the City Commission approved a suspension, Winter Park Voice reported on Sept. 28, 2026. The suspension will run for at least 90 days while the department writes a new policy, which Volkerson said may not be ready until early 2027. The city's Flock contract covers three cameras at a cost of $7,500 a year, and the report notes the Florida Department of Transportation revoked permits for such devices across the state in August.
