Placer County probation asks supervisors for a $3,437,286 Tyler Technologies amendment that adds AI to its case management software

From the archive (Dec 9, 2025): the department would co-develop and evaluate AI capabilities for supervision with the vendor, a county memo says.

Placer County's Probation Department asked the Board of Supervisors to approve an amendment to its Tyler Technologies case management contract that establishes a new five-year term and adds artificial intelligence and analytics supervision as well as mobile capabilities, according to a county memo dated December 9, 2025. The amendment would set a new five-year maximum of $3,437,286 and a maximum aggregate of $5,395,898 for the life of the agreement, which the board first approved on October 26, 2021. Under the amendment, Probation and Tyler would co-develop and evaluate AI capabilities for probation case management and supervision, and officers would get a mobile app with a voice-to-text feature that records field notes into the case management system. The memo is a request; this brief did not confirm the board's vote.

What we know

  • The memo asks for a new five-year maximum of $3,437,286 and an aggregate of $5,395,898 (Placer County memo).
  • Probation and Tyler would co-develop and evaluate AI capabilities for supervision (Placer County memo).
  • A mobile app's voice-to-text feature would record field notes into the case management system (Placer County memo).

What remains unknown

  • Whether and when the board approved the amendment.
  • What the AI features do in practice and how they are checked.
