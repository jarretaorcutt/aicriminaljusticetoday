Santa Barbara's city council voted to end its Flock contract; replacement Verkada cameras are delayed for community feedback

From the archive (Oct 9, 2026): the Daily Nexus reports a Flock camera also remains at UC Santa Barbara.

The Daily Nexus reported on October 9, 2026 that the Santa Barbara City Council voted to end its contract with Flock Safety on Aug. 23. The police department had planned to install Verkada license plate recognition cameras as an alternative, but the council decided to delay replacement cameras to get community feedback. The page says a Flock camera has been in use at UCSB's Santa Catalina Residence Hall for over a year, and that the Associated Students Senate passed a resolution condemning Flock camera use.

What we know

  • The council voted on Aug. 23 to end the Flock contract (Daily Nexus).
  • Replacement Verkada cameras were planned and then delayed for community feedback (Daily Nexus).

What remains unknown

  • Whether the council will approve replacement cameras.
