Cuyahoga County extends AI monitoring of jail phone calls to 2028

From the archive (Sep 22, 2026): The council added up to $2,695,757.68 for LEO Technologies' Verus software, paid from the Commissary Fund.

Cuyahoga County Council adopted Resolution R2026-0236 at its September 22, 2026 meeting, extending its contract with LEO Technologies for Verus software to 8/31/2028 and adding up to $2,695,757.68, according to the resolution, which lists Nays: None. The resolution says the project delivers communication monitoring, investigative analytics and AI-driven insights, and is funded from the Commissary Fund. Before the vote, Ideastream reported that a chief deputy told council the system captured 88 confessions in the second quarter of 2026 and helped solve 23 homicides and prevent 30 suicides; those figures come from the sheriff's office and were not independently checked.

What we know

  • The council extended the Verus contract to 8/31/2028 with up to $2,695,757.68 more (Resolution R2026-0236).
  • The project is funded from the Commissary Fund (Resolution R2026-0236).
  • The sheriff's office credited the system with 88 confessions in one quarter (Ideastream).

What remains unknown

  • How the sheriff's office counted confessions, homicides solved and suicides prevented.
