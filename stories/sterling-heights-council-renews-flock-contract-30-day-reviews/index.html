Sterling Heights, Michigan, renews its Flock contract and moves usage reviews to every 30 days

From the archive (Oct 21, 2026): A councilman who voted no wanted to make officer misuse of the data a local misdemeanor.

The Sterling Heights City Council voted to renew its contract to use Flock cameras after hours of deliberation, CBS News Detroit reported on Oct. 21, 2026. Police Chief Andrew Satterfield told the council that system usage will be reviewed every 30 days rather than every year and that the department has no history of misuse of the camera system. Councilman Michael Radke voted against the renewal and proposed making it a local misdemeanor if an officer misuses the data; the majority of the council objected to the idea, according to the report.

What we know

  • The council voted to renew the Flock contract (CBS News Detroit).
  • Usage reviews move from yearly to every 30 days, the police chief said (CBS News Detroit).

What remains unknown
