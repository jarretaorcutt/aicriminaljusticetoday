Lafayette's council strips Flock camera funding from its 2027 budget while nearby Erie keeps its 24 cameras for now

From the archive (Oct 21, 2026): Lafayette voted 5-1; Erie shortened its data retention from 30 days to seven days.

Twenty-six attendees took the podium during public comment before Lafayette's vote, according to Yellow Scene. Erie's police department shortened its data retention period from 30 days to seven days, and approximately $67,000 remains in Erie's proposed 2027 budget for the program, the magazine reports.

What we know

  • Lafayette passed a budget that completely stripped funding for the Flock contract (Yellow Scene).
  • Erie operates 24 Flock cameras and cut data retention from 30 days to seven days (Yellow Scene).

What remains unknown

  • Whether Erie will renew its contract, expected to come up in 2027.
