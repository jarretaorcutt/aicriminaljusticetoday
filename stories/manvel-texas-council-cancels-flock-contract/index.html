Manvel, Texas, cancels its Flock contract and covers all 16 cameras within 48 hours

From the archive (Oct 13, 2026): In nearby Pearland, about 63% of searches over a year came from out-of-state agencies, a crime analyst said.

Manvel City Council voted unanimously on Sept. 8 to cancel its four-year contract with Flock Safety and removed funding for it from the fiscal 2026-27 budget, Community Impact reported on October 13, 2026. All 16 of Manvel's Flock cameras were covered within 48 hours. "It's time to deflock Manvel," Mayor Dan Davis said. A Pearland police crime analyst told Community Impact that Flock's artificial intelligence analyzes the images for details such as a plate, color and body style, and that in Pearland about 63% of searches over the previous year came from out-of-state agencies. Manvel had not chosen a replacement.
