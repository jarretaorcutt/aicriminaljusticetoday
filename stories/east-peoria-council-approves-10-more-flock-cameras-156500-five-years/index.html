East Peoria's council approves a five-year, $156,500 contract adding 10 Flock license plate readers after public pushback

From the archive (Oct 7, 2026): The police chief says searches now need a report number and audits happen every 30 days, WCBU reports.

WCBU reported that the East Peoria City Council voted unanimously on Oct. 6, 2026 to approve $156,500, paid over five years, for 10 additional Flock automatic license plate readers. Police Chief David Catton told the station the city has required a report number for searches since its program began in 2022 and that audits moved from quarterly to every 30 days. WCBU also reported that a former Creve Coeur police chief is under Illinois State Police investigation over an allegation he used Flock to track his ex-wife. Catton declined to say which agencies have data-sharing agreements with East Peoria.
