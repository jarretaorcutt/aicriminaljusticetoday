Dutchess County's district attorney and sheriff move to phase out 64 Flock-style cameras as Poughkeepsie's mayor objects

From the archive (Sep 27, 2026): The county legislature's majority also planned a vote to end the cameras' use.

Dutchess County District Attorney Anthony Parisi and Sheriff Kirk Imperati announced they would begin rescinding Flock contracts and dismantling the network of 64 Flock or Flock-style cameras deployed in the county, Mid Hudson News reported on September 27, 2026. The county legislature's Democratic majority planned to vote on a resolution to terminate the cameras' use. The report says about $500,000 in Flock contract costs appeared in the DA's 2027 budget submission and that no evidence of misuse has been identified in Dutchess County. Poughkeepsie Mayor Yvonne Flowers said she was frustrated and that the county needs to discuss what will replace the system.

What we know

  • The DA and sheriff announced a phase-out of 64 Flock or Flock-style cameras (Mid Hudson News, September 27, 2026).
  • No misuse had been identified in Dutchess County, according to the report (Mid Hudson News).

What remains unknown
