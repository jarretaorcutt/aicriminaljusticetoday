Police Scotland demonstrates live facial recognition as the biometrics commissioner repeats his call for a law first

From the archive (Nov 6, 2026): Scotland has not decided whether to adopt the technology.

The Scottish Police Authority and the Scottish Biometrics Commissioner joined the event, according to Biometric Update. The commissioner reiterated his call for primary legislation on police use of LFR, describing it as highly intrusive mass public surveillance. The outlet notes it is up to Scottish lawmakers to decide whether to follow that recommendation.

What we know

  • Police Scotland hosted an LFR demonstration at Tulliallan attended by Justice Secretary Neil Gray (Biometric Update).
  • The Scottish Biometrics Commissioner reiterated his call for primary legislation (Biometric Update).

What remains unknown
