EFF's review of Flock search logs finds police using plate-reader data for school residency checks, background checks and noise complaints

From the archive (May 26, 2026): School police in one Georgia district ran more than 375 searches, EFF says.

By the AI in Criminal Justice Today newsroom · Published November 23, 2026

Jurisdiction

According to EFF, school police at Buford City Schools in Georgia, which serves only about 6,000 students, ran more than 375 searches between January 2025 and March 2026. EFF also identified 26 agencies whose officers searched the data over noise complaints. EFF, an advocacy group, attributes the pattern to the absence of a warrant requirement.

What we know

  • EFF analysed millions of police searches of Flock ALPR data (EFF).
  • Buford City Schools police ran more than 375 searches between January 2025 and March 2026 (EFF).
  • EFF identified 26 agencies with searches tied to noise complaints (EFF).

What remains unknown

  • Whether the named agencies changed their search policies afterward.
