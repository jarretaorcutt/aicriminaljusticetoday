Penn State ends its Flock license plate reader pilot and removes the cameras from its main campus

From the archive (Oct 13, 2026): A senior vice president cited cost, return on investment and operational value.

Penn State is not renewing its agreement with Flock Safety, and the cameras are currently being removed from campus, Sara Thorndike, the university's senior vice president for business, told a faculty senate meeting, WPSU reported on October 13, 2026. The university had been piloting Flock license plate readers on the University Park campus. Thorndike cited a review of the system's cost, return on investment and operational value, and did not rule out other security systems.
