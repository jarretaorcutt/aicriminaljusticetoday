Thiruvananthapuram's Central Jail launches PRISM, an AI camera surveillance system built with Keltron to stop contraband

From the archive (Aug 17, 2026): The system watches inside the prison and around its outer perimeter, the Times of India reported.

An AI-based surveillance and monitoring system called PRISM (Prison Real-time Intelligent Surveillance & Monitoring) was launched at the Central Jail in Thiruvananthapuram in collaboration with Keltron, the Times of India reported on Aug. 17, 2026. Home minister Ramesh Chennithala inaugurated it. The system uses AI-powered camera surveillance inside the prison and around its outer perimeter, and one of its key objectives is to prevent the smuggling of narcotic substances and other prohibited items, the paper reported.

What we know
