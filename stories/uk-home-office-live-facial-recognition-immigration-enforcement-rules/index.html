UK Home Office sets rules for live facial recognition in immigration enforcement, including transport hubs

From the archive (Oct 13, 2026): Non-alert images are to be automatically and permanently deleted, Biometric Update reports.

The UK Home Office published guidance on how Immigration Enforcement uses live facial recognition, Biometric Update reported on Oct. 13, 2026. The policy covers operations beyond ports of entry, including transport hubs and joint deployments with police, and says images of people who do not generate an alert are automatically and permanently deleted. It sets a false alert rate that should generally be 0.1 percent or lower and a matching threshold of 0.64 for deployments led by Immigration Enforcement, according to the report.
