Congress gets competing plate-reader bills: a funding ban from Sanders, audit and deletion rules from Hawley

From the archive (Oct 6, 2026): A third, Republican House bill has drawn six GOP co-sponsors.

Sen. Bernie Sanders introduced the Ban Flock Act on October 2 with Sen. Jeff Merkley and Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez; it would prevent federal funds from being used to purchase ALPRs, Truthout reported on Oct. 6, 2026. Sen. Josh Hawley introduced the Stop Flock Abuse Act on September 30, which would require police to delete driver data within 10 days, with exceptions for active criminal investigations, rather than ban the cameras. A House bill from Rep. Thomas Massie has six Republican co-sponsors, according to the report.

What we know

  • The Ban Flock Act would bar federal funds for ALPRs (Truthout; sponsor press release not yet reviewed by us).
  • The Stop Flock Abuse Act would require deletion of driver data within 10 days (Truthout; Hawley release not yet reviewed by us).

What remains unknown

  • Whether any of the bills will get a hearing.
