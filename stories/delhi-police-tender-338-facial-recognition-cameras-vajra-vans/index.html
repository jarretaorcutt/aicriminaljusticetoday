Delhi Police seek 338 facial recognition cameras as a rights group questions AI-equipped riot vans

From the archive (Oct 20, 2026): The Internet Freedom Foundation says the vans' paperwork doesn't say what the cameras record.

Delhi Police are procuring new Vajra anti-riot vehicles fitted with multiple 4K cameras and an onboard computer for AI analytics, and the Internet Freedom Foundation says the paperwork does not say what the cameras record, Biometric Update reported on Oct. 20, 2026. A separate tender seeks 338 fixed and pan-tilt-zoom CCTV cameras equipped with facial recognition, with a budget of ₹15.66 crore (around $1.6 million). The network was reportedly ordered around Ramlila Maidan, and the government expects to award the five-year contract, according to the report.

What we know

  • A tender seeks 338 CCTV cameras with facial recognition for a budget of ₹15.66 crore (Biometric Update).
  • The Internet Freedom Foundation questioned the Vajra vehicles' camera and data specifications (Biometric Update).

What remains unknown

  • Whether either contract has been awarded.
  • Retention and access rules for the footage.
