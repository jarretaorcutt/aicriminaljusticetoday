A DuPage County grand jury indicts two former suburban Chicago officers over alleged misuse of Flock plate-reader searches

From the archive (Oct 5, 2026): One former Itasca officer faces 13 felony official misconduct counts; the charges are allegations.

A DuPage County grand jury on Sept. 29 indicted two former officers, one from the Itasca Police Department and one from West Chicago, over unauthorized searches of Illinois license plates in Flock Safety databases, Shaw Local reported on October 5, 2026. The former Itasca officer is charged with 13 Class 3 felony counts of official misconduct and eight counts of misdemeanor computer tampering; the former West Chicago officer faces one felony count of official misconduct and one misdemeanor count of computer tampering. Each could face up to five years in prison. Both turned themselves in, were released, and were expected in court on Nov. 16. The charges are allegations.
