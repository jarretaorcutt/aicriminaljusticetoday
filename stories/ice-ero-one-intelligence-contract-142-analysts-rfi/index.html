ICE plans a contract of more than $100 million to staff about 142 intelligence analysts across its enforcement operation

From the archive (Sep 24, 2026): A Sept. 18 request for information covers ERO headquarters, all 24 field areas and three targeting centers.

U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement posted a request for information on Sept. 18 for "ERO Overwatch and National Security Enforcement," a more than $100 million contracting effort to place approximately 142 intelligence research specialists across ERO headquarters, all 24 ERO field areas and three national targeting and support centers, Biometric Update reported on Sept. 24, 2026. The outlet says one of those centers processes about 1.55 million biometric and biographic queries annually, and that DHS anticipates an award in the second quarter of fiscal 2027. The statement of work does not say the analysts will themselves run facial recognition or fingerprint searches, the report notes.

What we know

  • ICE posted an RFI on Sept. 18 for about 142 intelligence analysts (Biometric Update).
  • DHS anticipates an award in the second quarter of fiscal 2027 (Biometric Update).

What remains unknown

  • Whether the analysts will use biometric systems directly.
  • Who will win the contract.
