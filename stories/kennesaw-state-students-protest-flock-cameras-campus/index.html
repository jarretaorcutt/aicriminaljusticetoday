Kennesaw State students protest about 49 Flock cameras on their two Georgia campuses

From the archive (Oct 13, 2026): A student group estimates the university spent $400,000 on Flock devices; the university did not comment.

Students at Kennesaw State University are protesting the university's Flock Safety cameras, VOX ATL reported on October 13, 2026. According to the student group DeFlock KSU, about 49 cameras are across both campuses and the university has spent an estimated $400,000 on Flock devices; those figures are the group's, not the university's. The article cites the Institute for Justice as reporting 83 instances of Flock device misuse in Georgia since 2023, and says that as of July 2026, Flock Safety had ended a pilot audio feature. The university's associate dean of students did not respond to requests for comment, and Flock declined an interview.

What we know

  • DeFlock KSU counts about 49 Flock cameras across both campuses (VOX ATL, October 13, 2026).
  • The group estimates $400,000 in spending on Flock devices; the university did not comment (VOX ATL).

What remains unknown

  • The university's own camera count, spending and response to the protest.
