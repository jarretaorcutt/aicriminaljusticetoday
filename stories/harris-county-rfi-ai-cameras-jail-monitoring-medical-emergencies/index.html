Harris County, Texas, asks vendors about AI-enabled cameras to monitor people in its jail and alert staff to emergencies

From the archive (Jul 30, 2026): County officials said the request for information is not a commitment to buy and no decisions have been made.

Harris County issued a request for information seeking input from companies that provide AI-enabled camera systems for the county jail, ABC13 Houston reported on July 30, 2026. Officials said the cameras could monitor inmate activity in real time, identify potential threats and alert staff to medical emergencies, and that the request is not a commitment to purchase the technology. The Harris County Jail has faced years of scrutiny over inmate deaths, staffing shortages and compliance issues, the station reported. County officials said no decisions have been made.
