Poll: most voters want plate-reader cameras limited or restricted

From the archive (Oct 22, 2026): 39% say use them only for serious crimes or emergencies; 25% say they pose too much privacy risk.

In a Noble Predictive Insights poll of 2,527 registered voters conducted Oct. 5-7, 39% said license plate reader cameras should only be used for serious crimes or emergencies, and another 25% said they create too much risk to personal privacy and government surveillance, The Center Square reported on Oct. 22. The margin of error is +/- 1.95%.

The article quotes Racine County Sheriff Christopher Schmaling saying his office will not join a system that appears to use public safety as a justification for mass surveillance.

What we know

  • 39% of voters say plate readers should be used only for serious crimes or emergencies (The Center Square).
  • 25% say the cameras pose too much privacy risk (The Center Square).
  • The poll surveyed 2,527 registered voters Oct. 5-7 (The Center Square).

What remains unknown

  • Whether any new limits on the cameras will follow.
