Waukegan police officer charged with using plate readers to track co-workers' cars

From the archive (Oct 20, 2026): He faces five felony counts of official misconduct; a department-wide database audit is underway.

An 11-year Waukegan Police Department officer was charged with five counts of official misconduct, each a Class 3 felony, the Lake and McHenry County Scanner reported on Oct. 20. He allegedly used license plate readers and police databases to track vehicles registered to his co-workers and people tied to them; an initial review identified numerous such searches.

The department launched a department-wide audit of officers' use of law enforcement databases, which remains ongoing, according to the report. The officer was scheduled to appear in court on November 3. A charge is an accusation, not a conviction.

What we know

  • The officer faces five Class 3 felony counts of official misconduct (Lake and McHenry County Scanner).
  • Prosecutors allege he tracked co-workers' vehicles with plate readers and databases (Lake and McHenry County Scanner).
  • A department-wide database audit is ongoing (Lake and McHenry County Scanner).

What remains unknown

  • Which plate reader system was involved; officials did not say.
