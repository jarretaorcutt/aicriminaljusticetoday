Georgia arrests a former police sergeant for misusing a Flock license plate reader system

From the archive (Oct 19, 2026): An internal audit at the Vidalia Police Department led to two counts of misuse of a license plate reader system.

The Georgia Bureau of Investigation arrested former Vidalia Police Department sergeant Santonio Wilcher, 37, on two counts of misuse of a license plate reader system and a violation of oath of office, the GBI announced on Oct. 19. The Vidalia Police Department asked the GBI to investigate on Oct. 14, 2026, following an internal audit of its Flock Safety system.

Preliminary information indicates Wilcher accessed the Flock system on multiple occasions for non-law enforcement purposes, the GBI said. He is no longer employed by the department. The investigation is ongoing, and the case will go to the Middle Judicial Circuit District Attorney's Office. An arrest is an accusation, not a conviction.
