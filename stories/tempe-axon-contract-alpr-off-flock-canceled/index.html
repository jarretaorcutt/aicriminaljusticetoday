Tempe cancels Flock, signs a nearly $7 million Axon deal that bars turning on plate readers, and weighs a full ban

From the archive (Sep 27, 2026): The council approved the five-year Axon contract 6-1.

Tempe is beginning the discussion to ban the technology altogether, according to the paper, and over 30 Flock cameras in the city were covered with trash bags. Mayor Corey Woods said he didn't want to continue with something that could cause erosion of public trust.

What we know

  • Tempe canceled its Flock contract on July 25 (Tempe Independent).
