ICE records show agents took in 50.6 million voter records and examined more than 120 nonprofits

From the archive (Oct 9, 2026): Records obtained through FOIA don't show the voter data entered Palantir's ELITE system; Palantir says it never did.

By late May, ICE's Homeland Security Investigations reported ingesting about 50.6 million voter records from the Department of Justice, according to internal records Democracy Forward obtained through FOIA litigation, Biometric Update reported on Oct. 9. Records released October 7 show HSI investigated more than 120 nonprofit organizations.

By May 25, 2026, the agency had initiated 418 voter-fraud cases, 318 of which had not been referred for prosecution, the report says. The records do not establish that voter data was transferred into Palantir's ELITE targeting system, and a Palantir spokesman said voter roll data has never been integrated into ELITE.

What we know

  • HSI reported ingesting about 50.6 million voter records by late May (Biometric Update).
  • HSI investigated more than 120 nonprofits (Biometric Update).
  • 418 voter-fraud cases were opened by May 25, 2026; 318 had not been referred for prosecution (Biometric Update).

What remains unknown

  • Whether any voter data reached Palantir's ELITE system.
