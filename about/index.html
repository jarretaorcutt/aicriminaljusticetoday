About

AI in Criminal Justice Today is an independent, global publication tracking how artificial intelligence is changing criminal justice systems, public institutions, legal processes, corrections, supervision, rehabilitation, and the lives of people affected by those systems.

What we cover

Policing and investigations, courts, sentencing and risk assessment, corrections, probation and parole, community supervision, reentry and rehabilitation, education and workforce programs inside prisons, public defense and prosecution, policy and legislation, civil rights and privacy, biometrics, research, procurement, and developments worldwide. We deliberately look past the most visible policing stories to the parts of the system that get less attention.

Our approach

We are neither for nor against AI. We report benefits and opportunities alongside costs, limits, risks and rights implications, and we separate confirmed facts from vendor claims, official statements, allegations and analysis.

Independence

This publication is not an official publication of, and is not endorsed by, any college, university system, sentencing commission, government body, or other professional affiliation of its founder. Relevant affiliations and conflicts are disclosed where they bear on a story.

Automation

We use AI tools to help discover, organize and draft coverage. Every published story must pass an evidence and risk gate; uncertain or high-risk items are held, not published. See our methodology.
