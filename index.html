Daily Briefing · November 23, 2026

Utah prosecutors drop an AI clone of a murdered woman's voice from a death penalty resentencing; South Wales face-scan data shows a 24% ethnic minority share.

Four verified developments today, led by sentencing. Weber County prosecutors told a Utah judge they are abandoning a plan to present Joyce Yost's 1985 testimony in an AI-cloned voice after ElevenLabs refused a license and no other provider would work with them. In Wales, FOI data shows ethnic minority people were 24% of those selected for South Wales Police's officer-initiated face scans in 2025, against 7% of the population; the force is reviewing its approach. A Texas federal judge denied a defendant's release before sentencing and said he will sanction defense lawyers over AI-generated motions, and Sweden's prison and probation service is recruiting with AI-Kai, a digital prisoner. Nevada: no new development today. From the archive: 13 verified briefs, including the Court of Appeal ruling that police must never use AI to write witness statements and Western Australia's final facial recognition trial figures.
