Daily Briefing · November 20, 2026

New York's NYPD watchdog finds facial recognition policy breaches and issues 17 recommendations; a state bill would make fabricated citations in rulings judicial misconduct.

Two verified developments today, both in New York. The city's inspector general for the NYPD found the department largely complies with the POST Act on facial recognition but broke its own policy in some instances, and in one log about 33% of searches were marked "no" for supervisor review. A state senator introduced a bill to treat knowingly or recklessly including fabricated legal authority in a ruling as judicial misconduct. No corrections, supervision or Nevada development was verified today. From the archive: 18 verified briefs from September and October, including ICE's $125 million data deal, competing plate-reader bills in Congress, and the UK's £75m police AI centre.
