Have you seen AI in Criminal Justice Today?

LA County Probation gave 10 Camp Afflerbaugh youth a day of AI training; Maricopa prosecutors send police an AI-disclosure template; Connecticut has no statewide police AI inventory.

Four verified developments from November 25, 2026, published in a catch-up edition after a pause. LA County Probation says 10 youth from Camp Afflerbaugh took a full day of AI and digital skills training. Maricopa County prosecutors sent police a template for disclosing AI in arrest reports after a court commissioner declined to find probable cause in two AI-assisted cases. A Journal Inquirer report says Connecticut has no general statewide inventory of police AI. The UK Home Affairs Committee opened a Technology in Policing inquiry. Second run: the ACLU and EFF appealed the dismissal of their challenge to San Jose's plate-reader network, and seven verified archive briefs cover local decisions on Flock license plate readers.
