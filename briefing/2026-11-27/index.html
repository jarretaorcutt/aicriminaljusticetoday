Have you seen AI in Criminal Justice Today?

At least 25 court cases in England and Wales hit by AI misuse, including a police officer's Copilot-written sentencing statement; New York bill targets judges' fake citations; Illinois sets up a court AI committee.

Three verified developments from November 24-27, 2026. The Guardian reports at least 25 cases in England and Wales affected by AI misuse in 2026, including a police-written victim personal statement made with Copilot. A New York Senate bill (S.10741) would treat a judge's knowing or reckless reliance on fabricated legal authority as misconduct. The Illinois Supreme Court created a standing 18-member Committee on Artificial Intelligence. Four archive briefs cover Flock plate-reader rulings and local decisions.

Guardian and Bureau of Investigative Journalism find at least 25 court cases in England and Wales affected by AI misuse in 2026

New York Senate bill S.10741 would make a judge's knowing or reckless reliance on fabricated legal authority professional misconduct

The bill's sponsor, Sen. Michelle Hinchey, says AI use by a judge would not by itself be misconduct.

Illinois Supreme Court creates a standing Committee on Artificial Intelligence with 18 members
